
Document Title: MediSync Nexus Prototype Safety Policy
Document ID: MSN-POL-001
Document Type: Project Safety Policy
Version: 1.0

Purpose:
This document defines the safety boundaries of the MediSync Nexus academic research prototype.

Data:
The current prototype uses simulated patient records. These records are created for software testing and do not represent real patients or verified clinical measurements.

Clinical Limitations:
The system must not be presented as a diagnostic tool. It must not independently prescribe medication, recommend a treatment plan, or claim clinical safety clearance.

Evidence:
When the system retrieves reference material, it should preserve the document title, source, and relevant passage so a reviewer can verify the information.

Missing Evidence:
If no relevant source is retrieved, the system should clearly report that evidence was not found. It must not invent citations or claim that a source supports an answer when it does not.

Human Review:
Outputs are for academic research and demonstration. They are not for clinical use. A qualified professional must independently review any clinical information before real-world use.
